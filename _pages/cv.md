## Research experience

**Neurorehabilitation & BCI · Explainable Deep Learning**<br>
Developed NeuroValid, a comparative evaluation of EEGNet, Vision Transformers, and Convolutional Transformers across isolated EEG frequency bands using Optuna. Applied Grad-CAM, saliency maps, and Representational Similarity Analysis to investigate artifact dependence. The thesis reports an **80.78% F1-score** for a CvT model on strictly filtered Mu-band data; results should be interpreted within the reported evaluation setting.

**Imagined speech decoding · joint project · current**<br>
Built preprocessing and artifact-mitigation pipelines, including ICA and ASR, for KARA ONE EEG data with facial EMG contamination. Contributed to an end-to-end data and feature pipeline and used SHAP-based inspection to assess a co-developed spectro-temporal Transformer.

**Research internship · National Brain Mapping Laboratory (NBML), Tehran**<br>
Hands-on work with EEG and fNIRS recording for neurocognitive assessment, experimental paradigms, and biological signal analysis.

**Medical imaging · bone age estimation**<br>
Implemented a Vision Transformer for hand X-ray regression on the Atlas dataset. The CV reports a 12% MAE reduction versus traditional CNN baselines.
