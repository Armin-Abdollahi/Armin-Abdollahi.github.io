This work investigates whether rehabilitation BCI classifiers rely on neural activity or correlated ocular artifacts. The analysis uses **Grad-CAM**, **saliency maps**, and **Representational Similarity Analysis (RSA)** to examine model behavior.

**Status:** Under review at *Biomedical Signal Processing and Control*.
