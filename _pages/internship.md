## EEG & fNIRS research internship

At the National Brain Mapping Laboratory, I gained hands-on experience with multimodal neural recording, EEG and fNIRS measurements for neurocognitive assessment, and biological signal analysis.

The internship connected computational work with the practical side of neural data collection, including experimental paradigms and neurofeedback settings.
