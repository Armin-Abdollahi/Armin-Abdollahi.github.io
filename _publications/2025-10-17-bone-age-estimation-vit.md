This project implemented a **Vision Transformer (ViT)** for bone age regression from hand X-ray images using the Atlas dataset. The CV reports a **12% reduction in mean absolute error** compared with traditional CNN baselines.

The work was presented as a poster at the **2nd International Congress on AI in Medical Sciences (AIMS)** in Tehran in May 2025 and at **IOAS** in Germany in August 2025. The IOAS poster was published in the conference proceedings.
