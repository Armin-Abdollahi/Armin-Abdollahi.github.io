## Armin Abdollahi

AI researcher and engineer working across brain–computer interfaces, EEG and human–computer interaction. I am interested in neural decoding, imagined speech, motor imagery, interpretable systems and the design of useful human-facing neurotechnology.

## Education

- **M.Sc. Artificial Intelligence**, University of Tehran · 2022–2025 · graduated with distinction · thesis defense 20/20.
- **B.Sc. Software Engineering**, University of Tabriz · 2018–2022.

## Experience

**AI Programmer & Signal Processing Specialist**, White Tree International Academy · November 2024–present.
Develop and deploy AI and deep-learning systems for NeuroAI and EEG/biosignal applications; analyze cognitive data; build machine-learning workflows; and integrate data pipelines and models with backend systems.

**Founder, CEO & Chief Architect**, SyncNeurons.
Lead platform and neural-network architecture, with a focus on real-time EEG processing, explainable AI and zero-trust data architecture.

## Research & scholarly work

- Subject-independent motor-imagery decoding · under review after major revision at *Journal of Neuroscience Methods*.
- Imagined-speech decoding and few-shot personalization · manuscript in preparation; planned submission to *Expert Systems with Applications*.
- Bone-age estimation from hand X-rays with a Visual Transformer · accepted.
- Persian translation of *Brain–Computer Interface Research: A State-of-the-Art Summary* (Vol. 12), originally published by Springer.

## Selected applied work

EEG analysis and reporting software · cognitive assessment games with performance analytics · SyncNeurons neurotechnology platform.
