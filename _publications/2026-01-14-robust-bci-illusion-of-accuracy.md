This work evaluates EEGNet, Vision Transformers, and Convolutional Transformers across isolated frequency bands. The thesis reports an **80.78% F1-score** for a CvT on strictly filtered Mu-band data; this is a result from that specific evaluation setting.

The project uses Optuna for model search and explainability and representational analyses to inspect model behavior.

**Status:** Under review at *Biomedical Signal Processing and Control*.
